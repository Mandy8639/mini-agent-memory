# Known Trap: HIPAA BAA Status

## Overview
This page documents HIPAA Business Associate Agreement (BAA) requirements and status to ensure proper handling of healthcare client inquiries.

## What is a BAA
- Business Associate Agreement required under HIPAA
- Necessary for handling Protected Health Information (PHI)
- Must be in place before PHI can be processed

## Current Policy
- Details to be documented from company policy and legal terms

## Common Issues
- Healthcare clients may assume BAA is automatically in place
- Not all service tiers may include HIPAA compliance
- BAA must be signed before certain features can be used

## How to Handle
- Verify if client has healthcare/PHI requirements
- Check if BAA is executed for the account
- Escalate to legal/compliance if BAA needed
