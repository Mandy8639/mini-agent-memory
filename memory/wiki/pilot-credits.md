# Known Trap: Pilot Credits

## Overview
This page documents pilot program credits and promotional offers to prevent confusion about billing and credit application.

## Current Policy
- Details to be documented from sales and contract terms

## Common Issues
- Pilot credits may have expiration dates
- Credits may only apply to specific services or features
- Customers may not understand credit application terms
- Credits may not renew automatically

## How to Handle
- Verify if account has pilot credits
- Check credit expiration date
- Review terms of credit application
- Clarify what services credits apply to
