# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy — Current
Effective: 2025-01-01
Owner: People Ops

Full-time employees receive 20 days of PTO per calendar year.
PTO accrues monthly at 1.67 days per month.
Unused PTO may carry over up to 5 days into the next year.
Requests must be submitted at least 5 business days in advance via the HR portal.
Managers approve requests within 2 business days.
